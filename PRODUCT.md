# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web.

## Users

Auto parts distributors, repair and modification shops, and vehicle owners checking a part before requesting a quote.

## Product Purpose

Help buyers identify a catalog part, check its vehicle and specification details, and send a fitment-led inquiry for sourcing and export coordination.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public catalog includes headlights, tail lights, exhaust systems, and forged wheels. Body kits are sourced by inquiry, not sold from an inventory catalog.
- Use photos from the matching product record. Unavailable or uncertain images must not be replaced with generated SKU photos.
- List specifications, certifications, and availability only when supported by the record. Ask buyers to confirm order-critical unknowns.
- The homepage, admin, existing URLs, indexability rules, and inquiry delivery contract are outside the public interior redesign scope.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing catalog records, company and buying guides, brand assets, and the approved public interior redesign brief. Specifications and media availability vary by model.
